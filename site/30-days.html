30 days of landing pages

A random photo. A landing page. Sixty minutes from picture to finished design, every day for a month.

Every day, a randomly selected photo became the whole brief.

  • 30days in a row, one new page each day
  • 1random picture, chosen for me, as the starting point
  • 60minutes from picture to finished landing page

From picture to page, with the clock running.

  1. Read the photo

    Mood, colours and subject: what could this picture be selling?

  2. Invent the brand

    A name, a promise and a tone of voice that fit the image.

  3. Design the page

    Hero, navigation, type and call to action, built around the photo.

  4. Stop

    When the hour is up, the page is done. No second pass.

19 of the 30, in three loose themes

Nature and travel

When the photo is a landscape, the page steps back and lets the light do the work.

Culture, craft and lifestyle

Brands with a point of view, where typography carries as much personality as the photo.

Apps and services

Product pages, where a single photo has to explain what the product does.

A time limit turns taste into decisions.

  • Commit early

    No time for ten directions. Pick one, then spend the hour making it good.

  • Let the image lead

    Palette, type and tone of voice came from the photo, not from a template.

  • Range over routine

    From brutalist collage to quiet Nordic editorial: a new visual voice every day.
